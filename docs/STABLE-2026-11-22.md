## What works on the device

- Desktop, wallpaper, icons and controls render without the earlier color
  blocks; wallpaper is visible.
- Charging and battery percentage are published after restoring
  `firmware/adsp.mbn` in the addon; ADSP reaches the PMIC GLINK path.
- Wi-Fi works alongside charging.
- `CA_FORCE_TIMER=1`, `CA_DISABLE_DISPLAY_IDLE=1`,
  `CA_DISABLE_FRAMEBUFFER_COMPRESSION=1`, `CA_FORCE_FP16_FRAMEBUFFER=0` and
  `CA_DISABLE_DIRTY_REGIONS=1` are present in the first-boot backboardd
  environment.
- The Metal plugin is rebuilt with a 167-path `__DATA,__primelist`
  (13,172-byte section); the prime cache is persisted in Data.

## Known limitations

- The UI is still not a stable 120 fps. DPU reports 121.4 Hz, but moving
  segments measured roughly 41-115 fps, usually 50-80 fps.
- Removing `CA_FORCE_TIMER`, forcing all CADisplayLinks to full rate, and
  runtime hooks for `CADisableMinimumFrameDurationOnPhone` /
  `UIScreen.maximumFramesPerSecond` did not lift the 60 fps ceiling and the
  injection route can black-screen SpringBoard; those experiments are not
  part of this checkpoint.
- The on-screen keyboard does not appear in any app. `kbd` and `InputUI`
  start and report three input modes, but the keyboard window is not
  presented. InputUI injection caused black screens/soft restarts and was
  abandoned.
- Audio output is not implemented: the CS35L41 pair is present in the live
  device tree but no CS35L41/AudioReach output driver is installed, so volume
  controls have no audible/HUD effect.
- No partition was written. The device returns to fastboot/TWRP by hardware
  keys; recovery/ram logs were read through TWRP ADB.
